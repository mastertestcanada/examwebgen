Specify Seat Labels

Use this when you want specific seat numbers, or alphanumeric seats such as A4, B6 or Row H, Seat 5. Without labels the seats are numbered 1 to the number of students. Labels are only used when Randomize seats is ticked; typing them ticks it.

  • Separate seats with commas: A1, A2, A3.
  • Or put each seat on its own line. Then a seat may contain commas, such as Row H, Seat 5.
  • You need at least one seat per student. Repeated labels count once; spare seats stay empty.
