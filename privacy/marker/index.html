MasterTest Marker – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

MasterTest Marker (“the app”) is published by Jonathan Graham (“MasterTest”, “we”). The app lets teachers mark printed MasterTest answer cards with their phone’s camera. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

Everything the app reads stays on your phone. The app has no account, no advertising and no analytics. It connects to the internet only to check a MasterTest Pro licence or subscription, and then sends nothing about your exams or students. We receive exam information only if you choose to email it to us.

Information the app stores on your phone

When you scan an answer key and answer cards, the app stores, in its private storage on your phone and encrypted with a key that never leaves the phone’s secure keystore:

  • the exam: its title, questions’ correct answers and point values, read from the answer key’s QR code;
  • for each card scanned: the student’s name, student number and seat as printed on the card, the answers read, the score, any corrections you make, and a straightened image of the card;
  • your settings: the last email address you sent grades to, your sort order and your grade file format.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else by the app. It stays on your phone until you delete the exam or uninstall the app. Files prepared for an email (a grade file, a correction report) are kept in the app’s temporary storage, which Android clears when space is needed and which is removed when you uninstall the app.

The camera

The app uses the camera only while a scanning screen is open, to read QR codes and answer cards. Only a straightened image of each card is kept, with the scan it belongs to, inside the app’s encrypted storage; the camera photo itself is not kept. Nothing is added to your photo gallery.

Information you choose to send

The app can prepare two kinds of email. In both cases it opens your own mail app with the message filled in; nothing is sent until you press Send, and the message is delivered by your mail provider, not by us.

  • Grades. A CSV file of student names, student numbers and scores, sent to the address you enter (usually your own, to import into Canvas). We do not receive it.
  • Correction reports. When you correct a card by hand you may send the correction to support@mastertest.ca, to help us improve card reading. The report contains the exam title, the card’s serial number, the answers the app read, your corrections, and an image of the card with the student’s name, number, seat and QR code blacked out. No original photo is ever attached; the app does not keep one. The report is attached as a single file encrypted on your phone so that only MasterTest can open it; your mail provider and anyone else who sees the email cannot read it, and the email’s subject does not name the exam.

We use correction reports only to diagnose and improve how the app reads cards. We do not sell them or share them with anyone. We delete them once they are no longer needed for that purpose, and in any case within 12 months. To have a report you sent deleted sooner, email support@mastertest.ca.

MasterTest Pro and licence checks

The free version needs no connection. When you activate a licence code or a MasterTest Pro subscription, and about once a week after that, the app checks it with the MasterTest licence service, which runs on Google’s servers (Google Apps Script). Each check sends:

  • a random install id that the app creates for itself, which identifies this installation of the app and nothing else;
  • the licence code you entered, or the Google Play purchase token of your subscription.

The licence service records, for each code or subscription, the install ids of the phones using it (so a code can be limited to a number of phones), its expiry date and the date of the last check. It asks Google Play whether a subscription is active; payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy, and we never see your payment details. We use this information only to provide MasterTest Pro and to prevent codes from being shared beyond their limit. We keep it while the licence or subscription is in use and for 12 months after it ends. To have it deleted sooner, email support@mastertest.ca with your code or Google Play order number.

Backups

The app’s data is never included in Android’s backup to your Google account and is not copied when you move to a new phone. Its encryption key exists only in your phone’s secure keystore, so the stored files cannot be read anywhere else. If you change phones, export the grades you need first. You can also turn on an app lock in Settings, so the phone’s fingerprint, face or PIN is needed to open the app.

Student information

The app is a tool for teachers. Students do not use it, and it is not directed at children. Teachers who use it are responsible for handling their students’ information as their school or board requires. Because student information never leaves the teacher’s phone unless the teacher sends it, MasterTest does not hold any student records.

Deleting your information

  • Delete an exam in the app to remove it with all its scans and card images, or use Settings to delete exams automatically 30, 90, 180 or 365 days after they were added, or to delete all data at once.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Email support@mastertest.ca to have a correction report you sent us deleted.
