MasterTest Marker – Deleting Your Data

MasterTest Marker, published by Jonathan Graham (MasterTest)

MasterTest Marker has no user accounts. Almost everything it stores is on your own phone, and you can delete it there at any time. MasterTest holds only two kinds of record, and you can ask us to delete them by email.

Data on your phone

Exams, scanned cards, card images, names, student numbers, answers and scores are stored only on your phone, encrypted. To delete them:

  • One exam: open it in the app and delete it. Its scans and card images go with it.
  • Everything: in the app, open Settings and choose Delete all data.
  • Automatically: in Settings, choose to delete exams 30, 90, 180 or 365 days after they were added.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, including its encryption key.

We never receive this data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Data MasterTest holds

Email support@mastertest.ca with the subject “Delete my data” and say which of these you want deleted:

We delete them within 30 days and reply to confirm. Deleting an active licence’s record ends MasterTest Pro on the phones using it. Google Play keeps its own record of a subscription purchase under Google’s privacy policy; cancel the subscription in Google Play to stop it renewing.
