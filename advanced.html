MasterTest Web

MasterTest Generator in your browser: turn a Canvas quiz export into printable exams, answer cards and an answer key for MasterTest Marker. Everything runs in this browser tab: your quiz and class list are never uploaded.

Everything is optional. Each setting matches a command-line option of the desktop program, shown beside its name.
